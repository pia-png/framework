About Framework

STEM should be a place for everyone.

Framework is a youth-led nonprofit creating accessible, hands-on STEM experiences that help students explore, create, and discover where they belong.

Our story

Curiosity over competition.

STEM can feel intimidating when students are expected to compete, specialize, or already know what they want to do. Framework starts somewhere different: curiosity.

We create opportunities for students to explore STEM through real problems, creative challenges, and hands-on projects without needing prior experience.

Our goal is simple: help students discover that they can be problem-solvers, builders, creators, and innovators and that there is a place for them in STEM.

What we believe

Four ideas guide everything we do.

These principles shape how we design our programs, work with students, and build our community.

Accessible to Every Child

STEM opportunities should not depend on where a student lives.

Curiosity Over Perfection

Questions and experimentation matter more than getting it right.

Create, Test, Improve

Real learning happens when students build and iterate.

Everyone Belongs

Every student deserves to feel like they belong in STEM.

Our approach

Learn by doing.

Framework programs are designed around exploration rather than instruction alone. Students get the chance to make something, test an idea, learn from what happens, and try again.

01 — Explore

Start with a question, problem, or idea worth investigating.

02 — Build

Turn ideas into something tangible through hands-on work.

03 — Improve

Test, reflect, troubleshoot, and make the next version better.

04 — Belong

Share what you made and learn alongside a supportive community.
