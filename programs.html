What we build

Programs designed to be built, not sat through.

Every format shares the same DNA: a real problem, hands-on materials, and a showcase where every student presents what they made.

Flagship · 8 weeks

Discovery Challenge

Students work through a real engineering or science challenge including clean water, future cities, medical innovation, robotics over the course of 8 weeks. We end with a Celebration Showcase.

Single topic

STEM Discovery Workshops

Workshops in robotics, coding, biomedical engineering, and architecture. Often paired with a guest speaker working in the field.

5-day intensive

Framework Intensives

A five-day immersive design challenge built for community organizations, shelters, and summer programs — ending in a showcase of its own.

Evening event

Family STEM Night

An interactive evening where students and families build, experiment, and solve challenges side by side — learning that continues past the classroom.

Early learners

STEAM Play Day

Designed for younger learners: simple engineering, building, and play-based activities that make discovery the whole point.

Take-home

STEM Discovery Kits

Take-home kits with materials, instructions, and extension activities so the learning keeps going after the workshop ends.

STEM Pathways

The journey doesn’t end at the showcase.

Every student leaves with ways to keep exploring. We keep students updated with robotics clubs, science museums, coding platforms, STEM camps, local programs, age-appropriate scholarships, and future Framework events.
