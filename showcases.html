Showcases

We end every program with a celebration.

Students display prototypes, explain their design process, and demonstrate what they built to families and their community.

"Every participant is recognized."
How a showcase works

From first sketch to standing ovation.

1

Design

Students research a real problem and sketch their first approach.

2

Build

Prototypes take shape with hands-on materials and lots of iteration.

3

Test

Students test, fail, adjust, and improve the real heart of engineering.

4

Celebrate

Families and the community gather for the Celebration Showcase.

Why we do it this way

Confidence grows from being seen, not ranked.

Ranking students against each other tells most kids they're not good enough. A showcase tells every kid: you built something real, and people want to see it.

That's the whole idea behind Framework — curiosity and effort matter more than getting it "right" on the first try.
